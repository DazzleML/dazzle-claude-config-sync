## Caveats

A kill proves the suite noticed *this* change, not that it tests the right thing -- and **a clean sweep is evidence about the mutations tried, not proof of suite completeness.** The facade is thin; the vendored code it wraps was not swept (it is upstream's, tested upstream, and `_VENDORED.md`'s drift test pins its bytes rather than its behaviour).
