## Caveats

A kill proves the suite noticed *this* change, not that it tests the right thing -- and **a clean sweep is evidence about the mutations tried, not proof of suite completeness.** Eighteen mutants over the whole module; the fresh-context generator's palette (boundary operators, truthiness, dropped normalization, comparison/boolean flips, swapped arguments, removed guards) does not exhaust every way this classifier could be wrong, and no mutant targeted the module's dataclasses' field ordering or the `RUNGS`/`rung_of`/`token_of` round-trip directly (that pairing was already covered by `test_the_four_rungs_round_trip_between_token_and_number` and drew no mutant from the generator).
