## Caveats

A kill proves the suite noticed *this* change, not that it tests the right thing -- and **a clean sweep is evidence about the mutations tried, not proof of suite completeness.** Eighteen mutants over the changed regions of three files; the transport's locators, its executable search and its streaming reader were not in scope.
