## Caveats

A kill proves the suite noticed *this* change, not that it tests the right thing -- and **a clean sweep is evidence about the mutations tried, not proof of suite completeness.** Eighteen mutants over the changed regions of ten files; the transport's pre-existing lines (the roster, the completion call) were not in scope. One observation outside the sweep's verdicts: a refused credential (HTTP 401 on the model listing) reads as `no model server at ...` -- the transport's pre-existing wording, noted for a later unit.
