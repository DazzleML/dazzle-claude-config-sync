## Anomalies

None in either round's report; every restore verified, the four targets byte-identical to the aside copies afterwards. One flake outside the harness: `test_merge_ai.py::test_accept_in_the_same_run_as_the_copy_still_asks` failed once in a full-file run with `PermissionError [WinError 5]` on `os.replace` of the record's temp file, and passed four times in a row in isolation immediately after -- a transient Windows file-replace denial, not a code path. Worth a retry in `airecord.write` if it recurs.

## Caveats

A kill proves the suite noticed *this* change, not that it tests the right thing -- and **a clean sweep is evidence about the mutations tried, not proof of suite completeness.** Twenty mutants over the changed regions of four files; the recipe's older report branches, the diff-tool launch itself and the dossier were not in scope.
