## Anomalies

None in either round's report; every restore verified. One finding OUTSIDE the harness, from a killing test's first run (M17 above): the copy-mode branch reached a parent repository through git's upward search. `aisandbox.prepare` now gates the worktree attempt on the checkout being a repository itself; the home repository's worktree list was checked and is clean (the test run's worktree had been removed by `release`).

## Caveats

A kill proves the suite noticed *this* change, not that it tests the right thing -- and **a clean sweep is evidence about the mutations tried, not proof of suite completeness.** Twenty mutants over two new modules and three small additions; the prompt's wording and the record's older paths were not in scope.
