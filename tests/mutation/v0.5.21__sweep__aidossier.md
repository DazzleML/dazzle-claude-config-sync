## Caveats

A kill proves the suite noticed *this* change, not that it tests the right thing -- and **a clean sweep is evidence about the mutations tried, not proof of suite completeness.** The dossier's sentences are pinned by substrings; how the whole reads to a model is the slice checklist's eyes step, and how it reads on the maintainer's real checkout is his.
